The course contains two exams TKT200095 and CSM132045.
Both exams are examinarium exams (akvaariotentti), meaning that
the student needs to book a specific date within the given time window.
The general instructions for registering for the exams can be found [here](https://studies.helsinki.fi/instructions/article/taking-exams-examinarium-electronic-exam-rooms).

These are Helsinki Open University exams, meaning that they are free for
University of Helsinki students while carry a fee for others. Check open university pages for the current fee.


The exams are electronic but offline.
The exams are _seen_, meaning that the exact and complete question bank can be found in the course material.
Out of the shown questions, the exam consists of 6 questions, randomly selected for each student. 

The exams are graded pass/fail.
The acceptance threshold for passing grade is 75% of all available points.

The first exam (TKT200095) is organized primarily during the second period, and exam week between periods 3 and 4.
The second exam (CSM132045) is organized primarily during the fourth period, and intensive period before period 1 (starting from 2027).

The questions rely either on the course material or on the course book
_Security Engineering: A Guide to Building Dependable Distributed Systems, 3rd Edition by Ross Anderson, ISBN: 978-1-119-64281-7_.
The book can be bought either from Wiley or Amazon. It is also available for [free](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/archive/rja14/book.html) from the author's webpage.
The e-version of the book is also available via Helsinki University library.


Whenever the question will rely on the course book, the chapter is indicated in the material.
